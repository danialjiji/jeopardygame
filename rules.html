Jeopardy Quiz

Game Rules

1
Each team must appoint one team leader.
2
When it is your team's turn, the team leader will choose one category and one point value from the game board. The higher the points, the harder the question will be!
3
Once the question appears on the screen, each team will have 30 seconds to discuss and 10 seconds to answer. A countdown timer will be displayed on the screen.
4
All answers must be given in the form of a question, beginning with "What," "Where," "Who," "When," or other appropriate question words.
Example If the correct response is Hospital, the team should answer:
"What is a hospital?"
5
If the selected team gives an incorrect answer, the question will be opened to the surrounding teams for one opportunity to steal.
Example If Team Balenciaga is unable to answer the question, only Teams Valentino, Cavali, Versace or Dior have the oppoturnity to steal. The 4 team leads will compete to be the first to raise their hands, and the person who are fastest get's to steal answer for their teams.
6
If no team provides the correct answer during the steal opportunity, the question will be closed and no points will be awarded.
🚫

Important Notice

The use of mobile phones, smart devices, AI tools, or any other electronic devices to search for answers is strictly prohibited throughout the quiz. Please place all your gadgets in the middle of the banquet table.
